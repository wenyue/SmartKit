# Authoring Decisions

## Keep comment coverage contextual

Preserve purpose, contract complexity, and local reading experience as joint evidence rather than imposing a public-symbol documentation quota or treating neighboring absence as permission to omit. Either shortcut would replace a reader-centered judgment with a proxy and lose important one-caller contracts or useful simple-code omissions.

## Keep examples optional and policy in the entry

The examples teach information selection through stated contexts; their language, snippets, and formats do not establish facts or conventions for another project. Ordinary comment work already has the operative policy in the entry, so requiring the examples on every invocation would add reading without resolving a universal decision.
