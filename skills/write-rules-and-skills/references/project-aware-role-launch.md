# Project-Aware Role Launch

This concrete evidence policy applies to every public authoring run. Roles receive the common
runtime in `role-launch.md` and inspect the current repository directly within their frozen read
grants. No intermediary interprets or relays project evidence.

## Discover authoritative project evidence

Authors and Reviewers may inspect the exact Candidate resources, governing Rules, accepted Issues
or Specs, owned implementation, tests, configuration, and repository conventions admitted by the
Run Contract. Evidence acquires authority from its established owner and provenance, never from
mere presence in the repository or host envelope. Nonnormative context documents remain outside
the evidence basis unless a governing contract permits their narrow use.

Freeze every initial read path or narrowly owned path class before role launch. A role may request
an eligible Context Supplement or exact access expansion through the common bounded-update
contract. An unavailable eligible request preserves `CONTEXT_REQUIRED` or `ACCESS_REQUIRED`; an
out-of-envelope request requires a new aligned run.

## Bind project-aware operations

Authors receive Candidate operations only through the exact current Authoring or Repair Scope.
Reviewers remain read-only. Machine commands and Acceptance effects remain with their public stage
owners and do not enter Author or Reviewer grants. Every operation is reported and audited through
the common Role Launch contract.
