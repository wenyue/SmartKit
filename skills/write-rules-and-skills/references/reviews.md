# Quality and Correctness Reviews

Design freezes each contract's complete fresh cohort, responsibility assignments, and batching
schedule before any role or Candidate change. Stage entry activates frozen identities within `P`;
it adds no schedule fact.

Every Reviewer is persistent through its unit's correction loop and receives the same complete
Candidate Version, supplied evidence, and frozen evidence-selection policy and grants. No Reviewer
receives the Author's reasoning, intended repair, diff, or private research notes, or another
Reviewer's work. Reviewers first judge privately, then discuss each owned finding only with the
resident Author through the common direct correction lifecycle. Author and Reviewer independently
assess claims, reasons, support, and provenance until their bilateral fixed point. Neither
deference, voting, Controller interpretation, nor another Reviewer decides the outcome.

## Quality

Quality is the first proof stage. Freeze one cohort of four. Give every member the complete
Candidate resource tree, loading and distribution routes, accepted requirements, preserved
obligations, selected model, writing guidance, supplied evidence, and the same frozen read and
network grants.

Bind one Reviewer as primary owner of each responsibility below. For every responsibility, bind at
least one secondary Reviewer and make the remaining members supporting. Every Reviewer still
covers all four responsibilities:

- **Primary:** actively falsify the assigned responsibility with multiple high-risk
  counterexamples.
- **Secondary:** inspect every relevant occurrence and supply a counterexample or materially
  better alternative where one exists.
- **Supporting:** scan the complete surface and inspect more deeply wherever evidence warrants it.

### Artifact architecture and information flow

Primary: boundaries or ordering that conceal relationships, fragment one concern, couple unrelated
concerns, force backtracking, inflate context, or make change fragile.

Secondary: entry sufficiency, disclosure beside its trigger, route discovery, single-source
ownership, and loading or distribution paths. Supporting: Markdown that obscures phases, branches,
or peer relationships.

### Language, composition, and clarity

Primary: sentence and paragraph construction, or meaning dependent on implication, Author context,
or an ambient glossary.

Secondary: terminology, triggers, preconditions, exceptions, qualifiers, and the force of
requirements, permissions, prohibitions, and recommendations. Supporting: avoidable indirection or
tangled prose.

### Agent comprehension and usability

Primary: relationships among purpose, owner, boundary, sequence, and outcome that a fresh capable
Agent must invent; missing discriminators; and routes requiring unsupported inference.

Secondary: unclear inputs, authority, tools, observable actions, or evidence that confuses
completion, recovery, blocked, failure, and stop. Supporting: high-risk constraints placed away
from the behavior they govern.

### Economy and maintainability

Primary: text consuming material attention without changing behavior, evidence, authority,
boundary, or exit; avoidable semantic overhead; and fragile synchronized edits.

Secondary: duplicate meaning, unsupported constraints, and stale cached facts or dependencies.
Supporting: smaller consolidations with a concrete benefit.

Quality emits only `critical` or `material` findings. Each finding demonstrates a supported
present cost and names the improvement mechanism, bounded direction, affected scope, and
preservation constraints. Preference, symmetry, naming taste, file length, repair size, and numeric
shrinkage establish no defect.

A Quality Reviewer passes only when no critical or material claim remains on the current Candidate
Version and every note routed to that Reviewer is resolved. A later-stage note does not block
Quality. Quality passes only when all four members independently pass the same version. If a later
change invalidates the closed stage, bind a wholly fresh four-member cohort.

## Correctness

Correctness begins only after Machine `PASS` or valid `NOT_REQUIRED`. Freeze one cohort of two and
bind one scope to each Reviewer. Give both the accepted human-decision context, preserved
obligations, supplied governing evidence, selected model, current stage evidence, and the same
frozen read and network grants. Each independently inspects every relevant surface, exercises
supported scenarios, and tries to falsify the Candidate.

### Spec Fidelity and Semantic Integrity

Find an intolerable supported case where the Candidate:

- omits, substitutes, weakens, or violates an accepted requirement, obligation, change, non-goal,
  meaning, protection, behavior, or outcome;
- adds unsupported behavior, obligation, dependency, permission, or interpretation;
- moves meaning, responsibility, permission, or capability outside its evidenced owner,
  applicability, dependency closure, or representative context; or
- makes the same conditions require materially incompatible behavior.

### Critical Behavioral Integrity

Find an intolerable supported case where:

- a trigger, dependency, permission, action, validation, recovery, or stop fails on a required
  critical path;
- an Agent can assume authority, bypass a decision owner, exceed accepted effects, or violate
  safety;
- success, blocked, failure, recovery, or terminal state lacks an essential exit or evidence; or
- coincident conditions conflict, evade priority, or allow completion while work remains.

Correctness emits only `critical` findings. Each finding supplies one intolerable scenario,
supported evidence with its owner and provenance, concrete counterexample, material impact, repair
mechanism and bounded direction, affected scope, and preservation constraints. Repair size does
not determine impact, and the Author owns all replacement prose.

A nonblocking improvement may become a nonsemantic Scope Transfer Note to Quality and does not
alter its originating verdict. Each Correctness Reviewer passes only when no blocking claim remains
on the current Candidate Version. Correctness passes only when both independently pass that same
version. If a later change invalidates the closed stage, bind a wholly fresh two-member cohort.
