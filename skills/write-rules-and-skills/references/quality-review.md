# Quality Review

Start two fresh Reviewers in parallel and keep each one through its correction loop. Give both the
complete Candidate Version, accepted requirements, preserved obligations, candidate model, and
applicable writing guidance. Give neither the Author's reasoning, intended fixes, or a diff.

## Information Architecture, Completeness, and Clarity Reviewer

Check entry sufficiency, hierarchy, progressive disclosure, branch placement, terminology,
Markdown structure, resource routing, local clarity, and whether every necessary part is present.
Find content whose organization or omission makes an Agent miss, invent, or repeatedly reread
information even when the individual sentences are true.

## Semantic Economy, Minimality, and Redundancy Reviewer

Check whether every instruction earns its place. Find stale, duplicate, no-op, misplaced, overly
prescriptive, or environment-cached material. A removal or consolidation is valid only when it
preserves required meaning, choices, authority, safety boundaries, representative actions, and
exits. Do not require numeric shrinkage.

Apply the common Correction Cycle independently to both scopes. A Reviewer may transfer a
correctness concern but may not decide it. Quality Review passes only when both Reviewers report no
finding worth fixing for the same Candidate Version and every note routed to the Quality pair is
resolved. A note owned by a later stage follows the Correction Cycle queue and does not block
Quality PASS.
