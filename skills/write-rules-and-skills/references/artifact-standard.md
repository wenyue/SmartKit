# Shared Judgment Basis

An **artifact** is one Rule or Skill; its **Candidate** includes scoped supporting resources. Controller, Author, and Quality use this basis for allocation, writing, and quality judgment. Their role contracts determine actions and verdicts.

## Meaning and comprehension

Accepted authoritative requirements define intended meaning; existing Candidate text supplies regression evidence. Retain consequential obligations, conditions, exceptions, non-goals, and rationale that helps the reader act. Keep discarded options and authoring debate in the evidence record, including when the artifact itself asks its user to compare alternatives.

Correctness, Agent comprehension, and Agent execution matter equally. Make consequential decisions explicit and leave ordinary methods and immaterial detail to a capable Agent. Fix sequence when outcomes depend on it. Judge the complete reading path by whether readers can understand and carry out the intended work, without quotas for headings or length.

## Ownership and loading

Installed SmartKit core governance owns applicability, strength, precedence, and composition. Refer to that owner. When an intentional task alternative or project delegation affects a supported decision, state its condition, intended departure or delegated outcome, and remaining constraints.

### Choose the owner

| Owner | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rule | Bounded persistent policy across relevant work. |
| Task Skill | A triggered job with a bounded outcome. |
| Rule-led Skill | Policy applied to existing work through native Skill discovery. |
| Code, configuration, schema, or another active owner | The facts it governs. |

Give each policy one complete definition with the best existing owner by meaning. Always-loaded status establishes availability, not ownership. One public Skill can retain conditional resources without flattening their loading.

### Establish the route

Inspect related Rules and Skills, applicable always-loaded project and global Rules, and conditional Rules that may load together. Establish canonical sources, meanings, triggers, and supported routes by comparing their conditions, exceptions, and configuration. Current-session context alone cannot prove future availability.

- **From a Rule:** reference Rules or Skills. A Skill can rely on an independently loaded Rule only when that Rule has a supported independent loading guarantee everywhere the behavior is offered.
- **From a Skill:** invoke or reference Skills, including rule-led Skills. Use short owner pointers and local decision context without directly referencing independently loaded Rule files.

Preserve owner conditions and exceptions. Resolve ownership and loading before removing an obligation: a pointer alone proves neither applicability nor availability. Use canonical sources; editable caches and duplicates cannot establish authority. Missing source, access, or permission remains an owner dependency.

## Match form to work

A **Rule** leads with policy. Readers must be able to reconstruct its owner, Strength, observable conditions and outcomes, exceptions, and precedence.

A **Skill** follows `SKILL-MECHANICS.md` for packaging and invocation. Its description must support selection from the name, task, and context visible before loading. Keep discovery context economical while covering intended uses, nearby nonmatches, and easily missed necessary uses through static traces.

Choose a form around the work's decisions:

| Form | Organizing idea |
| --- | --- |
| Principle-led | Outcome, governing principles, consequential constraints, and completion; method stays open. |
| Procedure-led | Ordered steps where sequence or protocol affects correctness, safety, ownership, coordination, recovery, or external effects. |
| Rule-led | Requirements and exceptions governing relevant work. |
| Hybrid | An integrated combination whose consequential parts each satisfy their loading and execution criteria. |

Principle-led and procedure-led task Skills serve concrete jobs. Keep universally needed method accessible together; splitting only to shorten an entry adds indirection.

### Rule-led Skills

Use `rule-<domain>` names for this style, regardless of Strength. The model-facing description triggers on domain relevance before governed decisions and keeps explicit invocation and dedicated assessment discoverable. Declare default Strength in the entry and co-locate behavior-changing conditions with their requirements.

Normal activation constrains current work. Requested assessment covers existing work and reports its stated outcome under the same policy and the request's authority. Both routes stay within existing permission.

Broad triggering makes first-activation cost part of acceptance. Distinguish:

| Cost | What the reader loads |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Always-present description and pointers. |
| First activation | Entry and every unconditional read, including transitive dependencies and branches already written in the entry. |
| Later branch | Conditional content reached before a dependent decision or action. |

Design the entry to carry ordinary substantive changes and routine review through action and completion. Keep fundamental policy, Strength, and decision-sufficient evidence, conditions, and exceptions there. Retain context needed across all or nearly all work inline; disclose additional knowledge or constraints when recognizable facts trigger an owned resource. Load that resource before the dependent action or review judgment.

Trace ordinary changes, routine review, and deeper paths through actual files and transitive reads. Judge each branch by the knowledge needed, rather than the task label. Each read should supply needed context in time; a nominally conditional route used by almost all ordinary work defeats the first-activation goal. Use prior loading boundaries as regression evidence in revisions. Static traces suffice without task-frequency claims, separate reports, or runtime experiments; judge completeness and cost without quotas or forced fragmentation.

Core governance inheritance applies to owned normative resources. Illustrations and independently owned policies keep their own contracts. Native discovery and public exposure have separate owners; authoring an entry does not register it or migrate another policy.
