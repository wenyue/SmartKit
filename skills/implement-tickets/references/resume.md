# Pause and Resume

Read this when a prior attempt may exist, control was interrupted, evidence is missing or a material
blocker appears. Pause the whole batch at the first unproved boundary. Retain partial files, owned
local tails, candidates, accepted commits, claims and recovery evidence; leave later tickets
untouched. Continue only the original attributable work under the [main workflow](../SKILL.md).
A pause grants no automatic revert, reset, clean, discard, claim abandonment or queue change.

Describe an unproved prerequisite before its effect as `stopped`. Preserve an owner's returned
`failed` result after an attempt. Use `in-flight` while the original attempt may still run or its
effect is unresolved. Keep more specific owner vocabulary and independently proven facts alongside
later recovery. If continuation cannot be established, report the concrete blocker and exact next
owner/action.

## Reconstruct the original batch and its furthest phase

Use existing Git history, frozen selection/source revisions, tracker observations, check/review
reports, public-owner handoffs and observable runtime state. Create no batch journal. Recover the
facts needed to attribute the unfinished work:

- Scope, exclusions, dependency order, claim plan and exact worktree/branch binding.
- Immutable batch/unit bases, accepted prefix, current candidate, HEAD/tree and complete local-state
  ownership.
- Check provenance and acceptance bindings; original review inputs, role identities and axis
  attempt states; full reports or recovery locations, finding dispositions, repair deltas and
  Controller verification.
- Raw Worker, tracker, Create and finalizer results, observed effects/residuals, result-consumption
  state and the unfinished owner/action.

Claims can precede a worktree; finalizer receipts can outlive its removal. If evidence proves that
no batch effect occurred, retain that effect-free prerequisite result without claiming a batch
exists. Ambiguous scope, ownership, base, liveness or effects stops mutation.

An existing or possible finalizer attempt has priority until its owner returns control:

| Recovered state | Continue here |
| --- | --- |
| Finalizer response missing or finalizer possibly live | Recover the original finalizer boundary below. |
| Raw finalizer result not yet consumed | Enter [Consume the result](../SKILL.md#consume-the-result) once, including proven delivery with unfinished tracker closure. |
| Finalizer result already consumed | Resume only its named tracker, lifecycle, classification or other owner action, retaining proven effects without finalizing or consuming again. |
| Before worktree readiness | Recover claims and creation independently. |
| Before finalization | Recover the accepted commit prefix, at most one current ticket or batch-repair candidate, and its complete owned local tail; revalidate tracker/source facts and Worker control for that phase. |

### Re-establish the workspace binding

For an existing worktree, compare physical path, registration, Git common identity, branch, immutable
base relationship, HEAD/tree, index and staged/unstaged/untracked state with the retained phase.
Account for ignored state that affects ownership, writes, checks, finalization or cleanup. Give
bounded noncritical residuals an owner. Missing required identity or worktree presence stops at that
owner; a proven pre-creation phase can resume claim/Create gates without a worktree.

After a gap in implementation control, ask public `create-worktree` to re-evaluate that exact
existing worktree with retained state protected. Consume fresh `ready` evidence for the same
candidate. Reuse does not authorize replacement creation. Under continuous attributable control,
current identity/state checks suffice unless material drift invalidates readiness. Non-ready or
unresolved readiness stays with its owner.

## Recover claims and creation independently

Reconstruct the frozen claim plan, proven initial-claim prefix with raw responses and current proof,
any failed/in-flight claim with its before-state and intended delta, the untouched suffix, and
all deferred or no-claim routes. An unresolved claim belongs to neither the proven prefix nor the
untouched suffix. Use the tracker owner's original-operation observation/recovery route; creation
cannot follow an unresolved initial claim.

For every unresolved tracker effect, keep the original attempt, ticket, intended delta, before-state,
raw evidence, current observation and next owner/action. Acquired claims and proven effects remain
retained while that operation is resolved.

Establish Create's state separately:

- Proven `never-started`: evidence establishes no attempt and no worktree. Only this state, after
  every required initial claim, may enter clean creation.
- `non-ready-or-in-flight`: retain original effects and their recovery owner. Partial or interrupted
  creation returns to that owner.
- Attributable `ready`: retain its snapshot and establish its current binding before consuming it.

Refresh an existing candidate rather than replacing it. Progress only with current attributable
claims and readiness.

## Recover exclusive Worker control

Observe the original Worker through supported host interfaces. A missing response proves neither
failure nor quiescence. While it may write, preserve exclusive ownership: no replacement Worker,
review of a mutable candidate or overlapping implementation. Prove it stopped before resuming it or
assigning a replacement. A safe replacement inherits the exact candidate and owned local tail under
the original unit scope and review evidence.

Once quiescent, compare history and complete local state with accepted boundaries. Preserve the
accepted prefix. A commit or trailer cannot establish Controller acceptance; a marker written before
interruption remains unaccepted until every ticket predicate holds. Mixed or unexplained changes
do not become part of the current ticket merely because of their position.

## Recover the original review and acceptance

Recover immutable check/review inputs and complete original reports. Repeat missing checks when
needed, and observe the original reviewing roles through host interfaces. A proven never-started
axis may run within its original round on unchanged frozen inputs. A completed axis never repeats.
Missing reports after an attempt, or uncertainty about whether it ran, require recovery or pause
rather than a replacement formal review.

Before review freezes, incomplete attributable implementation can continue with its Worker. During
repair, recover unfinished scoped work, including returned but unaccepted results or a replacement
proved safe above. Continue [Controller closure](../SKILL.md#close-findings-until-the-controller-can-accept)
with the original reports, dispositions and candidate until accepted or blocked.

Retain raw failure or interruption alongside recovery through marker operations, source
reconciliation and finalizer returns. Resumption neither resets the formal round nor makes a Worker
return accepted. The same accepted-history and exact-state acceptance predicates still apply.

## Recover the finalizer boundary

Give the original host/session and public `finish-worktree` recovery route the exposed invocation
identity, frozen inputs and expected pre-state, accepted base/HEAD/tree, raw handoffs and observed
effects. That owner establishes liveness and recovers its original effect/result. Meanwhile preserve
source, target, publication, refs, receipts and recovery objects. Target contents or tracker status
alone cannot classify delivery or authorize another attempt.

The finalizer owns its external operation receipts; they remain compatible with this retained
handoff and the absence of a separate batch journal. While execution or effects are ambiguous, keep
the same `in-flight` attempt with the missing fact and next owner observation. Pass a recovered raw
result unchanged to the main consumption step, or resume only the named unfinished action for an
already consumed result. Never repeat a possibly completed tracker or finalizer effect to obtain a
receipt.
