# Pause and Resume

Read this branch for a possible prior attempt, interrupted control, missing evidence or material
blocker. Pause the whole batch at the first unproved boundary. Preserve partial files, owned local
tails, candidates, accepted commits, claims and recovery evidence; later tickets remain untouched.
Continue only the original attributable work under the [main workflow](../SKILL.md). A pause grants
no automatic revert, reset, clean, discard, claim abandonment or queue change. Report the concrete
blocker and exact next owner/action when continuation cannot be proved.

Use `stopped` for an unproved prerequisite before its effect, retain an owner's returned `failed`
result after an attempt, and use `in-flight` while the original attempt may run or its effect remains
unresolved. Keep more specific owner vocabulary and strongest proven facts alongside later recovery.

## Reconstruct the furthest phase

Use Git history, frozen selection and source revisions, tracker observations, check/review reports,
public-owner handoffs and observable runtime state; create no batch journal. Recover the scope,
exclusions, dependency order and claim plan; exact worktree/branch binding and immutable batch/unit
bases; accepted prefix, current candidate, HEAD/tree and complete local-state ownership. Retain
check provenance and acceptance bindings, original review inputs, role identities and axis attempt
states, full reports or recovery locations, dispositions, repair deltas and Controller verification.
Keep raw Worker, tracker, Create and finalizer results, effects/residuals, result-consumption state
and unfinished owner/action.

Claims may precede a worktree, and finalizer receipts may outlive its removal. Evidence that no batch
effect occurred supports an effect-free prerequisite stop, without claiming a batch exists.
Ambiguous scope, ownership, base, liveness or effects stops mutation. Give an existing or possible
finalizer attempt priority until its owner returns control:

- Recover a missing or possibly live finalizer response through the finalizer boundary below.
- Pass an unconsumed raw result once to [Consume the result](../SKILL.md#consume-the-result),
  including authoritative delivery with incomplete tracker closure.
- For a consumed result, continue only its named tracker, lifecycle, classification or other owner
  action, preserving proven effects without finalizing or consuming it again.
- Before readiness, recover claims and creation independently. Before finalization, recover the
  accepted commit prefix, at most one current ticket or batch-repair candidate and complete owned
  local tail, then revalidate tracker/source facts and Worker control for that phase.

For an existing worktree, compare physical path, registration, Git common identity, branch, immutable
base relationship, HEAD/tree, index and staged/unstaged/untracked state with the retained phase.
Account for ignored state affecting ownership, writes, checks, finalization or cleanup; attribute
bounded noncritical residuals to an owner. Missing required identity or worktree presence stops at
that owner. A proven pre-creation phase can resume its claim/Create gates without a worktree.

After a gap in implementation control, ask public `create-worktree` to re-evaluate that exact existing
worktree with retained state protected. Consume fresh `ready` evidence for the same candidate;
reuse does not authorize replacement creation. Under continuous attributable control, current
identity/state checks suffice unless material drift invalidates readiness. Non-ready or unresolved
readiness stays with its owner.

## Recover claims and creation

Reconstruct the frozen claim plan, proven initial-claim prefix with raw responses and current proof,
any failed/in-flight claim with its before-state and intended delta, untouched suffix and deferred
or no-claim routes. An unresolved claim belongs to neither prefix nor suffix. Use the tracker owner's
original-operation observation/recovery route; creation cannot follow an unresolved initial claim.
For any unresolved tracker effect, retain the original attempt, ticket, intended delta, before-state,
raw evidence, current observation and next owner/action. Preserve acquired claims and proven effects.

Establish Create independently as proven `never-started` with no attempt/worktree,
`non-ready-or-in-flight` with original effects and recovery owner, or attributable `ready` with its
snapshot. Only proven never-started creation after every required initial claim may start clean
creation. Partial or interrupted creation returns to its owner; an existing candidate is refreshed,
not silently replaced. Consume only current attributable claims/readiness before progressing.

## Recover Workers, reports and acceptance

Observe the original Worker through supported host interfaces. A missing response proves neither
failure nor quiescence. While it may write, preserve exclusive ownership: no replacement Worker,
mutable-candidate review or overlapping implementation. Prove it stopped before resuming it or
assigning a replacement. Any replacement inherits the exact candidate and owned local tail under
the original unit scope and review evidence.

Once quiescent, compare history and complete local state with accepted boundaries. A commit or
trailer alone cannot establish Controller acceptance; a marker written before interruption remains
unaccepted until every ticket predicate holds. Preserve the accepted prefix. Mixed or unexplained
changes cannot belong to the current ticket merely because of their position.

Recover original immutable check/review inputs and reports, repeating missing checks as necessary.
Observe the original reviewing roles through the host interface. A proven never-started axis may run
within its original round on unchanged frozen inputs. A completed axis never repeats. Missing
reports after an attempt, or uncertain attempt state, require recovery or pause rather than a
replacement formal review.

Before review freezes, incomplete attributable implementation may continue with its Worker. During
repair, recover unfinished scoped work, including returned but unaccepted results or a replacement
proved safe above. Continue [Controller closure](../SKILL.md#close-findings-until-the-controller-can-accept)
with original reports, dispositions and candidate until accepted or blocked. Keep raw failure or
interruption alongside recovery across marker operations, source reconciliation and finalizer
returns. Recovery neither resets the formal round nor makes a Worker return accepted.

## Recover the finalizer boundary

Give the original host/session and public `finish-worktree` recovery route its exposed invocation
identity, frozen input and expected pre-state, accepted base/HEAD/tree, raw handoffs and observed
effects. The finalizer establishes liveness and recovers its original effect/result. Preserve source,
target, publication, refs, receipts and recovery objects meanwhile; target contents or tracker status
alone cannot classify delivery or authorize retry. The finalizer's external operation receipts remain
its responsibility and are compatible with this retained handoff.

While execution or effects are ambiguous, retain the same `in-flight` attempt and missing fact/next
owner observation. Pass a recovered raw result unchanged to the main consumption step, or resume
only the named unfinished action for an already consumed result. Never repeat a possibly completed
tracker or finalizer effect to obtain a receipt.
