# Communication Quality

Adapt communication to its reader while preserving the facts, meaning, and operative boundaries.

## Shared Standard

Preserve factual, logical, domain, and contractual accuracy. Make material distinctions and
relationships explicit when they can change interpretation or execution. Include the relevant
conditions, required outcomes, qualifications, exceptions, ownership, and completion or stopping
conditions so the reader can apply the intended meaning without inventing a material premise.

Clear meaning can still leave room for judgment: several valid methods may satisfy the same
stated outcome and boundaries.

Use consistent terminology. Prefer clarity to brevity; shorten content only when required meaning
and boundaries remain intact. Communicate conclusions and material supporting evidence,
assumptions, and uncertainty without exposing private chain-of-thought.

## Audience Routing

In user-facing communication, use plain language appropriate to the user's technical understanding.
Briefly explain necessary technical terms while retaining distinctions that affect correctness.

When creating or materially editing an Agent-consumed document intended to instruct or constrain
an Agent's decisions or actions, apply `writing-for-agents`.

For artifacts serving humans and Agents, apply user-facing guidance to presentation and
`writing-for-agents` to operative content. A human-readable summary must not replace or weaken
the operative contract.
