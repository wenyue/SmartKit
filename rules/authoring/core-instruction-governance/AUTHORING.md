# Authoring Decisions

## Ownership survives delivery

Precedence follows the canonical owner of meaning, not the place an instruction happens to be loaded. Treating a copied or injected plugin artifact as project or conversation policy would let delivery mechanics silently elevate its authority.

## Compare in order, then preserve compatible requirements

Source, type, and specificity form an ordered comparison rather than competing signals. Keeping that order explicit prevents a detailed plugin procedure from defeating a higher-source constraint; limiting replacement to the conflicting clause preserves the rest of both contracts.
