# Authoring Decisions

## Separate readiness from permission

The workflow keeps these as independent conditions because either can be missing. A user instruction cannot make an unsupported method ready, and completed investigation does not authorize implementation. The decision table exposes the distinction without requiring a new approval when both conditions already hold.

## Carry authorization through the conversation

Questions during execution update the active plan rather than automatically reopening approval. The explicit-change and compatibility boundaries remain firm so continuity cannot silently become authority to change the accepted result.

## Keep ordinary lookup separate from artifact-producing research

A factual answer often needs a primary-source check, while the research Skill also creates a repository artifact and follows its own workflow. Conflating them would either discourage necessary verification or expand a small question into unrequested repository work.
