# Authoring Decisions

## Qualify portability without duplicating the public writer

The shared wrapper owns cross-project semantic qualification and closure, while the public workflow owns Candidate authority, writing, topology, validation, and judgments. A second private review protocol would create conflicting completion standards and evidence that the public result does not expose.

## Keep SmartKit policy with its qualifying owner

Keep the exact SmartKit dependency scope and Blueprint Target visibility in this Skill as a directly consultable reference. The public writer receives qualified policy input, so using it for another provider does not load SmartKit's maintenance workflow or require an unpackaged repository-local file. Generic relationship semantics remain public and reusable by standalone and Setup-driven project authoring; blueprints carry only their target-specific obligations.

## Choose representative targets by consequential differences

More project names do not necessarily establish more coverage. The portfolio is organized around behavior-changing seams, including unavailable capabilities and mid-path stops, so success in several similar environments cannot hide a missing failure or permission boundary.

## Bind closure to observable outputs on one version

Portability cannot be inferred from a public COMPLETE label or presumed hidden Reviewer work. Fingerprint-bound observable outputs make the wrapper's conclusion inspectable while preserving the public verdict and avoiding a second permanent Candidate or report.
